SELLER AND TUTOR TERMS

These terms apply on top of the Terms of Use when you sell on the 101 Marketplace or teach on 101 (paid courses, lessons or 1:1 sessions). You accept them when you open a shop or submit a tutor application.

1. Who can sell or teach

  • You must be 18 or older, and a learner or tutor account holder. Guests can buy but cannot sell.
  • We verify you before your first listing or paid course goes public: your identity, your payout account in your own name, and for businesses your registration with URSB [TODO: decide which documents]. We may ask again later.
  • You must have the right to sell what you list and to teach what you upload.

2. What you can’t sell

Anything illegal in Uganda; counterfeit or pirated goods, software or courses; exam leaks or graded-work services; weapons, drugs, alcohol, tobacco, adult content; personal data; financial products; anything aimed at harming children. We decide what may be listed.

3. Listings and courses

  • Everything you publish goes to our approval queue first. We check content, price, links and suitability for under-18s.
  • Describe your item truthfully: what it is, its condition, what’s included, delivery time and any limits. Show the full price in UGX including any delivery fee.
  • Tutors: you set a price and we approve it. We may make any course free or change its price after telling you. You are responsible for the accuracy of what you teach.
  • If you are a business, your listing must show your business name, physical address and contact details, as the Electronic Transactions Act s.24 requires [Advocate: confirm whether 101 can display this on the seller’s behalf].

4. Rights in your course content

  • You keep ownership of your courses. You give us a non-exclusive licence to host, stream, show, translate captions for, and promote them on 101 while they are listed, and to keep providing them to learners who already bought them.
  • You must own, or have written permission for, every video, book, image, code sample and music track you use. “Fair use for teaching” in Uganda is narrow; do not upload other people’s paid books or videos.
  • YouTube lessons must be videos you are allowed to embed. We embed them in privacy-enhanced mode.
  • If we receive a valid copyright notice we will take the content down, as the Copyright and Neighbouring Rights Act s.49A requires, and tell you. Repeated infringement ends your account.

5. Orders and delivery

  • Accept or decline orders promptly and deliver within the time you stated, and at most 30 days (Electronic Transactions Act s.27). If you can’t, tell the buyer and we refund them.
  • Digital items: deliver the file, licence key or access straight away.
  • Physical items: ship to the address given, use tracked delivery or a pickup point, and upload proof.
  • Only use the buyer’s details to fulfil that order. Never add them to a mailing list, sell them or contact them about anything else. Selling personal data is a crime (Cap. 97 s.37).

6. Getting paid

  • The buyer pays through our licensed payment provider. Money for your order is held by the payment provider until the buyer confirms delivery, or 72 hours after you mark it delivered with no dispute.
  • Then your share (price minus our fee) becomes available to withdraw after the holding period of [14] days [TODO: admin setting], which covers the buyer’s cancellation and refund rights.
  • Our fee is shown in your seller centre and on the Pricing page before you list. We tell you at least 30 days before we raise it.
  • Payouts go only to a mobile money or bank account in your own name. Minimum payout UGX [20,000] [TODO: admin setting].
  • You are responsible for your own taxes. We may withhold tax where the law requires and give you a statement.

7. Refunds and disputes

  • Buyers have the rights in the Refund Policy, including the 7-day cancellation right under the Electronic Transactions Act. You must honour them.
  • A dispute freezes the order’s money. We review evidence from both sides and decide within [10] working days. If we refund the buyer, the amount (less nothing for the buyer) comes from your order’s held funds; if those were already paid out, from your next payouts.

8. Ratings and reviews

Only buyers who bought, and learners who enrolled, can review. Don’t buy, swap or fake reviews.

9. Suspension

We may hide listings, hold payouts or close your shop if you break these terms, receive repeated valid complaints, or we suspect fraud. We tell you why and you can appeal.

10. Your responsibility

You are the seller or teacher of your item or course and responsible for it. You agree to cover our reasonable losses if a claim against us is caused by your listing, course or conduct.
