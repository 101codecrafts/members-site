COOKIE NOTICE

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. “Local storage” is a similar store. This notice lists what 101 uses.

Always on (needed for the site to work)

Name Set by Why How long
__session, __client_uat, __clerk_* Clerk (sign-in) keep you signed in securely session to 1 year [TODO confirm]
cookies (local storage) 101 remembers your cookie choice until you clear it
theme, lite, lang, cc.sidebar (local storage) 101 dark mode, data-saver, language, layout until you clear it
cc_user, onboarding draft (local storage) 101 role and sign-up progress on this device until you sign out or finish
cc_ai_* (local storage) 101 your 101 AI chat and voice settings on this device until you press Clear

Only if you choose “Accept all”

Name Set by Why
Google AdSense cookies (e.g. __gads, __gpi) Google show and measure ads on free accounts after the launch period. Never personalised for under-18s. Not set on checkout, sign-in, legal pages or for paid plans.

We do not use any analytics or tracking cookies today. If we add any, they will appear here and run only after “Accept all”.

YouTube lessons use youtube-nocookie.com (privacy-enhanced mode): YouTube does not set cookies until you press play.

Your choice

The banner lets you pick Essential only or Accept all. To change your mind, use Cookie settings in the footer [Engineering: add this link] or clear this site’s data in your browser.
