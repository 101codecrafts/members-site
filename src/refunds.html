REFUND AND CANCELLATION POLICY

This policy covers plans, courses, sessions and marketplace orders. It never reduces your rights under Uganda’s Electronic Transactions Act (ETA) or the Sale of Goods and Supply of Services Act. Any term that tries to remove those rights has no effect (ETA s.28).

1. Your 7-day right to cancel

You may cancel a purchase made on 101 within 7 days of receiving it (or of the agreement, for a service) and get a full refund within 30 days (ETA s.25). If you return a physical item, the only cost you may pay is the direct cost of sending it back.

How it works for each kind of purchase:

What you bought Your cancellation right
Plan (Standard, Plus, Pro) Cancel within 7 days of payment for a full refund. [Advocate: the ETA does not clearly say whether a deduction for days already used is allowed; we have chosen a full refund.] After 7 days you can cancel any time; the plan runs to the end of the period you paid for.
101 or tutor course Cancel within 7 days of buying for a full refund, as long as you have completed less than 25% of the lessons [Advocate: confirm this limit is lawful under ETA s.24(7)(d)/s.25; if not, remove it]. If a course is refunded, any certificate issued for it is cancelled.
1:1 session or paid event Cancel up to 24 hours before the start for a full refund. If the tutor or organiser cancels or doesn’t show, full refund.
Marketplace — physical item Cancel within 7 days of delivery. Return the item unused, in its packaging.
Marketplace — digital item (template, software, ebook) Cancel within 7 days unless you have already downloaded or activated it; if it doesn’t work as described, see section 2. [Advocate: confirm under ETA s.24(7)(g)-(h).]

2. Faulty, wrong or not delivered

Whatever the time limit above, you are entitled to a repair, replacement or refund if what you got is faulty, not as described, or not fit for its purpose (Sale of Goods and Supply of Services Act), and to a refund if it isn’t delivered within the time promised or 30 days (ETA s.27).

3. Buyer protection on the marketplace

Your payment is held by our licensed payment provider until you tap Confirm delivery, or 72 hours after the seller marks the order delivered if you have not opened a dispute. Before then you can open a dispute in My orders; this freezes the money while we review. Opening a dispute does not cut your 7-day cancellation right.

4. How to ask for a refund

  • My orders → Return / dispute for marketplace orders.
  • Settings → Billing → Request refund for plans, courses and sessions [TODO: confirm the screen].
  • Or email support@101codecrafts.org with your payment reference.

We reply within 2 working days and pay approved refunds within 14 days, and always within the 30 days the law sets, to the same mobile money number or card you paid with.

5. Payments that went wrong

Double charges, failed payments that took money, or amounts different from what you were shown are refunded in full as soon as we confirm them.

6. Under 18

If a child under 18 bought something without their guardian’s approval, the guardian can ask for a refund at support@101codecrafts.org.

7. Complaints

If you are not happy with our decision, reply to our email to have a second person review it. You can also contact the Uganda Consumers Protection Association or, for payment-provider problems, the provider’s own complaints desk and then the Bank of Uganda.
