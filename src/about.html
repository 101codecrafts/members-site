Our story

Why “101”?

In school, “101” is the first course in a subject: the place everyone starts. 101 Codecrafts is built around that idea. Nobody arrives knowing everything. You start with the basics and craft your way up, one project at a time.

Learning tech on your own can be confusing and lonely: scattered tutorials, no one to ask, nobody to build with. We started 101 Codecrafts in Kampala to put learning, building and people in one place: clear paths to follow, real projects to show for them, and peers and mentors who help you keep going.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Is 101 Codecrafts free?

Joining is free. During the launch month everything on 101 is free, with no ads. After that there is a free tier with ads, and 101 Pro if you want it ad-free. Some tutor-led courses and sessions have a price in Uganda Shillings, always shown before you pay.

Where is 101 based?

In Kampala, Uganda. Courses, the library, news and opportunities are online, so you can learn from anywhere, and members also meet in study groups and at events and meetups.

Who can join?

Anyone learning or working in tech, from Primary and O-Level to A-Level, university and professionals. No experience needed. Create a free account to start.

How do I become a tutor?

Create a free account, choose “Tutor” when you set up your profile, and pick the levels you teach. The 101 team verifies tutors before they can publish paid courses or sessions, and every course is reviewed before it goes live.

I’m under 18. Can I join?

Yes, with a parent or guardian’s consent. When you sign up we ask for a guardian’s details and send them a consent request. Until they agree, messaging and payments stay locked. Read more in the privacy policy.

How do I get a certificate?

Finish a 101 course (video lessons, notes and quizzes) to earn a 101 certificate. Each one has a code that anyone can check on the verify page. Free partner courses, such as freeCodeCamp or Coursera, give their own certificates; the courses page shows which ones are free.
