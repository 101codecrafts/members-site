About

ABOUT 101 CODECRAFTS

Start at 101. Craft your way up. 101 Codecrafts is a tech community based in Kampala, Uganda, for anyone growing in IT: future programmers, computer scientists, data analysts, ethical hackers and software engineers. Every skill level is welcome. You learn by building, alongside people who are learning too.

Our mission

Make the first steps into tech free, practical and less lonely. We point you to the best free courses, give you real projects to show for them, and connect you with peers and mentors who help you keep going.

How to join

  1. Create your free account. It takes a minute on the sign-up page.
  2. Pick a learning path. Start with one of the popular learning paths or browse all courses.
  3. Find your people. Join a study group, come to an event, or read how the community works.
