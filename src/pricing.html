QUESTIONS

How do I pay?

MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, or a Visa or Mastercard card, through a secure checkout. 101 never sees your PIN or card number. Right now every checkout is a test: no real money moves.

What happens to locked lessons?

On Free, the first lessons of each free 101 course (and any preview lessons) are open. The rest show a lock and stay visible, so you always see the whole course. Any paid plan opens them.

I bought a course. Does my plan matter?

No. A course you buy is fully yours, whatever your plan.

Can I change or cancel?

Yes. An upgrade starts when the payment is confirmed. A cancel or downgrade takes effect at the end of the period you paid for, and nothing you made is deleted.

How do tutors set course prices?

A tutor suggests a price within their plan's range, and a 101 admin approves it before learners see it. Admins can make any course free.
