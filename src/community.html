Community

LEARN. BUILD. COLLABORATE. GROW.

Start at 101. Craft your way up. 101 Codecrafts is a community of learners, builders and mentors based in Kampala, Uganda, from Primary to professional. Whether you're writing your first line of code or levelling up at work, you'll find people to learn with and things to build together. Joining is free.
