PRIVACY POLICY

Draft for legal review. Last updated: 2 November 2026. This policy explains how 101 Codecrafts ("we") handles personal data in line with Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 and the Data Protection and Privacy Regulations, 2021. We intend to register with the Personal Data Protection Office (PDPO) at NITA-U as a data collector/processor.

1. What we collect

  • Account data: name, email, phone number, date of birth, education level, role (learner or tutor).
  • Learning data: courses, progress, notes, submissions, certificates.
  • Community content: posts, comments, projects, bug reports and uploads.
  • Payment data: transaction references from Flutterwave and Mobile Money providers. We do not store full card numbers or Mobile Money PINs.
  • Technical data: device type, approximate location from IP, cookies (see below).

2. Why we use it (lawful basis)

  • To run your account and deliver courses (contract).
  • To process payments and tutor payouts (contract, legal obligation).
  • To keep the community safe, moderate content and prevent fraud (legitimate interest).
  • Optional analytics and marketing messages (your consent, which you can withdraw at any time).

3. Children (under 18)

Under section 8 of the Act we only process a child's personal data with the consent of a parent or guardian. Sign-ups under 18 trigger a guardian consent request; until it is given, messaging and payments stay locked. Groups for Primary, O-Level and A-Level learners are teacher-led and moderated.

4. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to processing, or ask us to stop direct marketing (sections 24–28 of the Act). Email privacy@101codecrafts.org; we respond within 30 days. You may also complain to the PDPO.

5. Sharing and transfers

We share data only with processors that help us run the service (hosting, email, payments, video) under written contracts. Where data is stored outside Uganda we make sure the destination gives adequate protection as section 19 requires.

6. Security and retention

Data is encrypted in transit, access is role-based and logged in our audit log. We keep account data while your account is active and delete or anonymise it within 12 months of closure, unless the law requires longer (for example financial records).

7. Cookies

Essential cookies keep you signed in. Analytics cookies are only set if you choose "Accept all" in the cookie notice; you can change this by clearing site data.

8. Contact

Data protection officer: privacy@101codecrafts.org, Kampala, Uganda.
