AI NOTICE

You are talking to an AI

101 AI is an automated assistant, not a person. It answers questions about 101 Codecrafts and explains news and lessons. It also powers some helpers such as news summaries.

It can be wrong

Answers are generated by a computer model and can be out of date, incomplete or simply wrong. Check anything important. It is not medical, legal, financial or career advice, and it does not decide anything about your account, marks, payments or applications. People do that.

What we send, and to whom

  • When you ask 101 AI something, we send your question and the last few messages of the chat to OpenRouter, a US company, which passes it to a free AI model (for example Meta’s Llama). We do not send your name, email or account details.
  • Free AI models may be run by providers who keep or learn from what they receive. So don’t type personal details (yours or anyone else’s), passwords, or anything private.
  • We do not store your chats on our servers. Your browser keeps the conversation until you press Clear. We keep a small usage record (date, which model, whether it worked) to stop abuse and to apply daily limits.
  • Voice: if you use the microphone, your browser’s own speech service turns speech into text; in Chrome this may send audio to Google.

Under 18

The assistant is told when the user is under 18. It keeps answers age-appropriate, never asks for personal details, and suggests talking to a parent, guardian or teacher about personal problems.
