Legal

REPORT AND TAKEDOWN

Report something unsafe

Use Report on any post, profile, message, listing or lesson, or email safety@101codecrafts.org [TODO confirm]. Tell us what it is, where it is (a link), and why it’s a problem.

  • A child at risk, sexual content involving a child, or a threat to someone’s life: we act first, review within 24 hours, remove the content, preserve evidence and report it to the Uganda Police. If someone is in immediate danger, call 999 or 112, or the Uganda Child Helpline 116 (free).
  • Harassment, hate, scams, phishing, malware, personal data posted without consent: reviewed within 2 working days.

We tell you what we did. The person reported can appeal.

Copyright notice

If you own the copyright in something on 101 that was posted without your permission, send a notice to copyright@101codecrafts.org [TODO confirm] with (Electronic Transactions Act, Cap. 99):

  1. your full name and address;
  2. your signature (typed or electronic);
  3. the right you say is infringed (what the work is and that you own it or act for the owner);
  4. the exact content on 101 (a link);
  5. what you want us to do (remove or block it);
  6. your phone number and email;
  7. a statement that you are acting in good faith; and
  8. a statement that the information in the notice is correct to your knowledge.

A false notice can make you liable for the loss it causes.

We remove or block access to the content promptly — normally within 2 working days — and tell the person who posted it, who may reply with a counter-notice. We also act on notices from the Registrar of Copyright (Copyright and Neighbouring Rights Act s.49A). Members who repeatedly infringe lose their accounts.

Law enforcement

Requests from the police, courts or regulators go to legal@101codecrafts.org [TODO confirm]. We disclose personal data only when the law requires it.
