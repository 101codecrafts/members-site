YOUR DATA

Under Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019 you can get a copy of the data 101 holds about you: profile, enrolments, progress, payments, posts and consents.

GUARDIAN (UNDER 18)

If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must agree before you can use paid features, 1:1 sessions or chat. We email them a secure link; nothing changes until they say yes.

DELETE ACCOUNT

This erases your profile, messages, posts, notifications and settings, and closes your sign-in. Payment records the law requires us to keep are anonymised. There is a 24-hour cool-off: you can cancel until then.
