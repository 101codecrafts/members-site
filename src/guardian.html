Guardian consent

A child wants to join 101 Codecrafts

You were named as the parent or legal guardian of a learner under 18. Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act (Cap. 97, s.8) says we need your consent before we keep their details or open their account. Draft wording pending review by a Ugandan advocate.

  • Right now we hold only their name and date of birth, and your name, contact and relationship to them. We got your details from the learner.
  • If you agree, their learner account opens and we also keep their school level, interests, goals, course progress and projects.
  • Until you agree, their account can't buy, book 1:1 sessions, post, message or rate.
  • Selling, payouts, a public profile, private messages with adults and personalised ads stay off for every learner under 18, even with your consent.
  • Their data is stored with our providers in Germany, France and the United States. 101 AI questions go to an AI provider in the United States without their name or email.
  • If you don't agree, or don't answer, we delete the request and their details.
  • You can ask what we hold, correct it, or withdraw consent at any time by emailing privacy@101codecrafts.org. Withdrawing pauses the account.

I don't consent

Thank you. Consent recorded. The learner's account is now active. We've emailed you a confirmation. To review what we hold or withdraw consent, email privacy@101codecrafts.org.
