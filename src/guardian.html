Guardian consent

A child wants to join 101 Codecrafts

You were named as the parent or guardian of a learner under 18. Under Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019 (s.8), we need your consent before their account opens.

  • We keep only their name, date of birth and your contact until you agree.
  • Until you agree, their account can't buy, book 1:1 sessions, post, message or rate.
  • Selling, payouts and a public profile stay off for every learner under 18.
  • You can withdraw consent at any time from the link in the confirmation email.

I don't consent
