Design system

CARD SYSTEM

One brutalist card for the whole site: a 2px outline, a hard offset shadow, square corners, and the same slots in the same order. Use a card for a summary that leads somewhere else; use a plain list when people scan or compare. Source: css/cards.css.

  1. Media .ck-media: fixed ratio 16:9 (or --1x1, --4x3), object-fit:cover, decorative unless it carries meaning.
  2. Eyebrow .ck-eyebrow: badge or category, one line.
  3. Title .ck-title: a real heading (h2/h3 to fit the page outline), clamped to 2 lines. It holds the one primary link, .ck-link, stretched over the whole card.
  4. Meta .ck-meta: source, date, level.
  5. Body .ck-body: supporting text, clamped to 3 lines.
  6. Footer .ck-foot: actions, pinned to the bottom. Secondary buttons sit above the stretched link and stay clickable. No links inside links.

When not to use a card

Homogeneous items that people scan or compare (prices, deadlines, names) read faster as a list or table with fixed columns. Settings, forms and filters are panels, not cards: they never lift or take a stretched link.
