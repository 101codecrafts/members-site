2. The short version

  • We collect what we need to run your account, your courses, the marketplace and payments.
  • If you are under 18, we keep only your name, date of birth and your parent’s or guardian’s contact until they say yes.
  • We never sell your personal data. Selling personal data is a crime in Uganda (Cap. 97, s.37).
  • Our servers are outside Uganda (Germany, France and the United States). We ask for your consent to that when you join.
  • You can download your data or delete your account from Settings at any time.

5. Children (under 18)

  • We ask everyone for their date of birth. Under-18s can join only as learners, never as tutors or sellers.
  • Under Cap. 97 s.8 we need a parent’s or legal guardian’s prior consent. We email them a one-time link. Until they agree we keep only the child’s name, date of birth and the guardian’s contact, and the account cannot buy, book 1:1 sessions, post, message or rate.
  • If the guardian does not reply within 14 days [TODO confirm], or says no, we delete the child’s account and the guardian’s details.
  • Even with consent, under-18s never sell, receive payouts, have a public profile, or send private messages to adults. Ads shown to them are child or teen treated and never personalised.
  • A guardian can see what we hold, change their decision, or withdraw consent at any time by emailing privacy@101codecrafts.org [TODO: or the link in their confirmation email, once built]. Withdrawing pauses the child’s account.

7. Data stored outside Uganda

Our providers store and process data in Germany, France, the United States and other countries. Uganda’s PDPO has not yet published a list of countries with adequate protection, so we rely on your consent (Cap. 97 s.19(b); Regulation 30). We ask for it separately when you join. We also choose providers with strong security and written data-protection terms. If you do not consent you cannot have an account, because the service runs on these providers.

9. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what data we hold and who it was shared with (s.24); we reply within 30 days, and decide within 7 days (Regulation 35);
  • correct or delete wrong, old or unnecessary data (s.16; Regulation 29);
  • stop processing that causes you substantial damage or distress (s.25);
  • stop direct marketing (s.26);
  • have a person, not only a computer, review a decision that significantly affects you (s.27);
  • withdraw any consent you gave, without affecting what was done before.

Most of this is self-service in Settings → Privacy: Download my data and Delete account. For anything else email privacy@101codecrafts.org. We may ask you to prove who you are.

If you are not happy with our answer you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Office, 7th floor, Padre Pio House, Plot 32 Lumumba Avenue, Kampala · info@pdpo.go.ug · https://pdpo.go.ug.

10. Security and breaches

Data is encrypted in transit, access is limited by role and recorded in an audit log, and database rules stop members reading each other’s private data. If personal data is accessed by someone who should not have it, we report it to the PDPO immediately, as Cap. 97 s.23 and Regulation 33 require, and we tell you when the PDPO directs us to or when it helps you protect yourself.
